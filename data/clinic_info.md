# Green Valley Family Clinic

Green Valley Family Clinic is a friendly neighbourhood clinic offering primary care for the whole family.
Edit this file any time - the bot reads it fresh on every question, no restart needed.

## Opening Hours

- Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed
- Public holidays: Closed (emergency line stays open)
- Last appointment of the day starts at 4:30 PM

## Location and Parking

- Address: 12 Healthway Street, Downtown, Springfield
- Landmark: Next to Central Pharmacy, opposite the City Library
- Parking: Free parking for patients behind the building (20 spaces)
- Public transport: Bus routes 12 and 47 stop 2 minutes away
- The clinic is wheelchair accessible (ramp at the main entrance and a lift inside)

## Services

- General check-ups and consultations
- Child vaccinations and growth checks
- Blood tests and basic lab work (results in 24 hours)
- Women's health and antenatal care
- Minor procedures (stitches, wound dressing)
- Diabetes and blood pressure monitoring
- Basic ECG

## Fees

- General consultation: $30
- Specialist consultation: $45
- Follow-up visit within 7 days: Free
- Basic ECG: $20
- Blood test packages: from $15

## Payments and Insurance

- We accept cash, debit/credit cards and mobile wallets.
- We work with these insurance providers: HealthFirst, CarePlus and BlueShield.
- Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID.

## Booking Appointments

- Appointments are 30 minutes long.
- You can book through this chat, by phone, or by walking in (walk-ins wait for the next free slot).
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to complete a short form.
- Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 4 hours before your appointment, by phone.

## What to Bring

- Photo ID and insurance card (if you have one)
- Previous medical reports or prescriptions
- A list of medicines you currently take

## Policies

- Lab tests that need fasting: no food for 8 hours before (water is fine).
- Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
- Masks are optional but welcome.
- The clinic does not give medical advice over chat. For emergencies, call the emergency line or your local emergency number.
